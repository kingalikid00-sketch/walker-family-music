Privacy Notice

This page describes the intended privacy approach for The Walker Family website. Before accepting customer orders, the business must publish a final notice identifying its legal operator and contact details.

Information customers may provide

An order may ask for a name, email address, occasion, musical preferences and story details needed to create the requested song.

How information is used

Information should be used only to respond to enquiries, prepare and deliver the requested song, provide order updates and meet legal obligations.

Payments

Payment details should be handled by the payment provider. The website should never request or store a customer's card number or security code in a story form.

Retention and contact

Customer information should be kept only as long as needed for the order and applicable legal requirements. Add a verified business contact address before launch.

Important: This is a draft notice, not legal advice. The final operator identity, contact address, retention periods and applicable privacy rights must be confirmed before launch.
